10: 2. Detailed audio subsystem design
11: 2.1 Dual-microphone array
12:
13: Place two silicon microphones at an acoustic spacing that forms an approximately uniform linear array.
14: Enable AEC3 noise suppression and automatic gain control.
15: Set the AEC tail length for indoor near- and mid-field conditions.
16: 2.2 VAD (Voice Activity Detection)
17: Use a two-stage cascaded VAD:
18: Front-end VAD: WebRTC VAD (GMM model), Aggressive=2, processing 10 ms frames.
19: Back-end VAD: secondary confirmation using an energy threshold and zero-crossing rate, with a 300 ms filter window.
